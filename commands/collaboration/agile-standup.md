# /agile-standup — Daily Standup

Prepare or facilitate the daily standup for the team.

If `$ARGUMENTS` is provided, treat it as the team name or sprint context.

## Structure (per team member)
1. **Yesterday:** What was completed?
2. **Today:** What is planned?
3. **Blockers:** Any impediments?

## Delivery Lead Focus
After the standup, briefly summarize:
- Open blockers and who resolves them
- Risks for the sprint
- Escalation needed (yes/no)

## Output Format

**Standup – [Date] · $ARGUMENTS**

| Person | Yesterday | Today | Blockers |
|--------|-----------|-------|----------|
|        |           |       |          |

**Delivery Lead Note:**  
- Blockers: 
- Risks: 
- Escalation: 

Ask for participants and sprint status if not available in context.
