# /research — Research & Analysis

Support the Delivery Lead with targeted research on a topic.

If `$ARGUMENTS` is provided, treat it as the research topic or question.

## Active Role
First, check `context/roles/active-role.md`:
- If a role is active, read the corresponding `context/roles/[name].md`
- Apply that role's **perspective**, **typical questions**, **frameworks**, and **output style** throughout the research
- Mention the active role at the top of the output

## Approach
1. Sharpen the question (what exactly needs to be answered?)
2. Identify relevant sources, frameworks, or best practices — filtered through the active role's priorities
3. Present findings in a structured way
4. Derive a clear recommendation from the role's perspective
